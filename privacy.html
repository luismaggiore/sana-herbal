Privacy promise

Your privacy, in plain words.

We treat every customer as part of the Sana family. Your peace of mind matters to us as much as your health, so we protect your personal information with the same care we put into choosing our ingredients.

1. What we collect

Information you share: your name, contact details and payment information. Nothing more.

Information collected automatically: our store platform records some technical data, such as your IP address and device type. We only see it as aggregated statistics.

2. How we use it

To process your orders, personalize your experience and, if you agree, keep you informed about new formulas and offers.

3. When we share it

Only when it's needed to provide our services, to comply with the law or to protect our rights. We never sell your data.

4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to make your visit easier and more personal. You can manage them in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to serve you, comply with the law or keep our community secure. After that, it's securely deleted.

6. How we protect it

We use technical and organizational measures to keep your information safe.

7. Children

Our products and services are intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and if we find any, we stop processing it.

8. Your rights

You can update, correct or ask us to delete your personal information at any time. Just reach out.

9. Contact

Write to us at care@sanaherbal.com or use our contact form. For full legal details, see our privacy policy.
