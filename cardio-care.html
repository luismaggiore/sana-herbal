About Cardio Care

Long-term care for your arteries

Artery health is built over years, not days. Cardio Care brings together four botanicals with published cardiovascular research: hawthorn, arjuna, ginger and cayenne.

Each one is used as a whole-plant powder, the way the formula's creator has always worked with them: Dr. Marco Sikaffy, a naturopathic doctor with decades of clinical practice. There are no isolated extracts, no fillers and no proprietary blend. Every milligram is on the label.

How to take it: 3 capsules a day, one with each meal. One 90-capsule bottle lasts a month.

What's inside

  • Hawthorn berry 148 mg

    Rich in flavonoids and OPCs. Studied in randomized trials for supporting healthy blood pressure and cholesterol levels.*

  • Arjuna bark 148 mg

    A traditional Ayurvedic heart herb. Its triterpenoids are studied for supporting healthy cholesterol and normal endothelial function.*

  • Ginger root 148 mg

    Its gingerols are studied in randomized trials for healthy blood pressure, cholesterol and triglyceride levels.*

  • Cayenne pepper 6 mg

    A small dose of natural capsaicinoids, studied for supporting circulation and healthy lipid profiles.*

Clinical research & science

Published research on the individual botanicals in Cardio Care. These studies were not conducted on the finished formula.

  • Procyanidin C1 from Hawthorn Modulates Metabolic-Inflammatory Axis to Combat Vascular Senescence in Atherosclerosis 2026, Food Research International
  • Effect of Terminalia Arjuna on Total Platelet Count and Lipid Profile in Patients of Coronary Artery Disease 2019, Advances in Human Biology
  • Polyphenolic Profile and Biological Activity of Chinese Hawthorn (Crataegus pinnatifida BUNGE) Fruits 2012, Molecules
  • The effect of ginger supplementation on lipid profile: A systematic review and meta-analysis of clinical trials 2018. Phytomedicine, National Center for Biotechnology Information
  • Effects of Capsicum annuum supplementation on the lipid profile in adults with metabolic syndrome: a systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs 2023, A. Hjazi et al.Capsicum supplementation significantly reduced total cholesterol (TC) by ~4.98 mg/dL.
  • Lipid-Lowering Efficacy of the Capsaicin in Patients With Metabolic Syndrome 2022, Z. Jiang et al.Capsaicin supplementation lowered TC and LDL-C in patients with metabolic syndrome.
  • Red pepper attenuates cholesteryl ester transfer protein and atherosclerosis 2003, M.J. Kwon et al.Red pepper reduced CETP activity (a marker linked with cholesterol transport) and slowed atherosclerosis in cholesterol-fed models.
  • Terminalia arjuna bark extract impedes foam cell formation and promotes macrophage/foam-cell apoptosis 2019, S. Bhansali et al.Arjuna extract inhibited foam cell formation by downregulating CD36 expression and promoted apoptosis in macrophages/foam cells.
  • Hawthorn extract alleviates atherosclerosis through regulating inflammation and apoptosis related factors 2019, S.Z. Wang et al.Hawthorn extract reduced inflammatory markers (MCP-1, IL-1β, hs-CRP) and modulated Bax/Bcl-2 expression to stabilize plaques.
  • Hawthorn leaf flavonoids attenuate atherosclerosis in ApoE-knockout mice by promotion of reverse cholesterol transport and foam cell inhibition 2020, A. Nazhand et al.Hawthorn flavonoids improved reverse cholesterol transport, inhibited foam cell formation, and reduced vascular lipid accumulation.
  • Exploring the mechanism of hawthorn leaves against coronary heart disease via network pharmacology and in vitro validation 2022, J. Ding et al.Hawthorn polyphenols suppressed MMP2, MMP9, IL-6, IL-1β and macrophage activation, consistent with plaque-stabilizing, anti-inflammatory effects.
  • The effects of Capsinoids and Fermented Red Pepper Products on lipid profiles: meta-analysis of RCTs 2022, M.R. Amini et al.Capsinoids (from red pepper) significantly reduced total cholesterol (TC) with mild effect on LDL, HDL, or TG.
  • A Randomized Controlled Clinical Study to Evaluate the Effect of Terminalia Arjuna (Add-On Medication) on Cardiac Function in Coronary Artery Disease 2024, International Research Journal of Multidisciplinary Scope
  • Crataegus laevigata decreases neutrophil elastase and has hypolipidemic effect: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial 2011, School of Medicine, University of Valencia, Valencia, Spain
  • Ginger Extract Consumption Reduces Plasma Cholesterol, Inhibits LDL Oxidation and Attenuates Development of Atherosclerosis in Atherosclerotic, Apolipoprotein E-Deficient Mice 2023, The Rappaport Family Institute for Research in the Medical Sciences
  • Capsaicin Attenuates Arterial Calcification Through Promoting SIRT6-Mediated Deacetylation and Degradation of Hif1α (Hypoxic-Inducible Factor-1 Alpha) 2022, AHA|ASA Journals
Quality & safety
Identity
Every herb is identity-verified before production, to confirm it's the exact species on the label. Cardio Care's botanicals come from named origins: hawthorn from Bulgaria, arjuna from India, and ginger and cayenne from the US.
Potency
Every batch is third-party tested by ISO 17025-accredited laboratories (Eurofins or Daane Labs). Not once per formula: every single batch.
Purity
Every batch is screened for heavy metals (lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury) and microbial contamination (E. coli, Salmonella, yeast and mold). If a batch fails, it never ships.
